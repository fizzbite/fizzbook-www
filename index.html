FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do my clients need to download an app?

No. Clients book from your link in any browser in about 20 seconds. They get a confirmation and reminders by text or email.

Can I move my clients and bookings over from another app?

Yes. Upload a CSV export of your client list and upcoming appointments, or send it to us and we'll do the import for you, free.

How do deposits work?

Choose a fixed amount or a percentage per service. Clients pay when they book and it comes off the final price. If they no-show, you keep it.

Do you take a cut of my bookings?

No. You pay a flat monthly price from £19. Standard card processing fees apply only when you take payments through FizzBook.

Does it work for mobile and home-visit pros?

Yes. Add travel time between appointments and a service area, and FizzBook only offers slots you can actually reach.
